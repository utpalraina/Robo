📊 What is Aggressor Ratio?

The aggressor ratio measures who is initiating trades in the market. When a market order hits the ask price, it's a buy aggressor. When it hits the bid, it's a sell aggressor.

📈 How to Use This

1️⃣ Confirm Trends: High buy aggression during uptrends confirms momentum
2️⃣ Spot Reversals: Divergence between price and aggression can signal reversals
3️⃣ Gauge Sentiment: Extreme ratios (>1.5 or <0.7) indicate strong conviction
4️⃣ Volume Weight: Large trades matter more than small ones
