What is ICT Confluence?

Confluence means multiple ICT factors aligning to give you a higher-probability trade setup. The more factors that align with your trade direction, the stronger the signal. Always verify these factors manually on your chart before entering a trade.

MS Market Structure (3 pts)

Identifies the trend direction by analyzing Higher Highs/Higher Lows (bullish) or Lower Highs/Lower Lows (bearish). Aligned structure means the market is moving in your trade direction.

Why 3 Points: Market structure is the foundation of ICT trading. Trading WITH the trend dramatically increases win rate. Going against structure is the #1 reason traders lose. This factor alone can make or break a trade.
How to Verify: On the 15m/1H chart, connect swing highs and lows. For LONG: look for HH+HL pattern. For SHORT: look for LH+LL pattern. Mark the last 3-4 swing points to confirm the structure.
PD Premium/Discount Zone (3 pts)

Divides price range into zones. Discount zone (below 50%) is ideal for LONGS - you're buying cheap. Premium zone (above 50%) is ideal for SHORTS - you're selling high.

Why 3 Points: Buying low and selling high is fundamental to profitable trading. Entering in the correct zone gives you better risk:reward and aligns with institutional behavior. Smart money buys in discount and sells in premium.
How to Verify: Draw Fibonacci from recent swing low to swing high. 0-50% = Discount (buy zone), 50-100% = Premium (sell zone). Check the Key ICT Levels card for current PDH/PDL values and calculate the 50% equilibrium.
OB Order Block (3 pts)

Areas where institutions placed large orders. Bullish OB is the last down candle before a strong up move. Price often returns to these levels before continuing the trend.

Why 3 Points: Order blocks represent institutional footprints - where big money entered. Price respects these levels 70%+ of the time. Trading at OBs means you're entering where institutions entered, with tight stop losses possible.
How to Verify: Find the last opposite-color candle before a strong move. For bullish OB: find the last red candle before green candles pushed up. Draw a box from that candle's high to low. Is current price at this box?
FVG Fair Value Gap (2 pts)

An imbalance zone where price moved so fast it left a "gap". Price often returns to fill these gaps. Trade when price reaches an FVG aligned with your direction.

Why 2 Points: FVGs show imbalance between buyers/sellers. While price often returns to fill them, they're not as reliable as OBs alone. FVGs work best as additional confirmation, especially when they overlap with order blocks.
How to Verify: Look for 3-candle patterns where the middle candle's body doesn't overlap with candles on either side. The gap between candle 1's high and candle 3's low (bullish FVG) or candle 1's low and candle 3's high (bearish FVG) is the FVG zone.
OTE OTE Level (3 pts)

Optimal Trade Entry - the 62%-79% Fibonacci retracement zone. This is ICT's "sweet spot" for entries. Combined with other factors, OTE provides excellent risk:reward.

Why 3 Points: The 62-79% retracement zone is where institutions typically add to positions. Entering here gives you the best risk:reward ratio - your stop is close (below the swing) but your target is far (the previous high/low). ICT considers this the "sweet spot".
How to Verify: Draw Fibonacci retracement from the impulse move's start to end. The zone between 0.618 and 0.786 levels is the OTE zone. For LONG entries: wait for pullback into this zone. Best when combined with an Order Block in the same area.
MSS Market Structure Shift (3 pts)

A break of market structure confirming trend change. Bullish MSS = break above recent swing high. Essential confirmation that smart money is entering your direction.

Why 3 Points: MSS is the CONFIRMATION that smart money has entered. Without it, you're guessing. A recent MSS in your direction proves institutions have committed capital. It's the difference between hoping and knowing the trend has shifted.
How to Verify: Mark the most recent swing high/low. Bullish MSS: price breaks and closes above the recent swing high. Bearish MSS: price breaks and closes below the recent swing low. The candle must CLOSE beyond the level, not just wick through.
LQ Liquidity Sweep (2 pts)

When price takes out obvious highs/lows (stop hunts) then reverses. Smart money hunts retail stops before the real move. A sweep followed by reversal is a strong signal.

Why 2 Points: Liquidity sweeps show smart money has finished hunting stops and is ready to move. It's a timing indicator - useful but not essential. A sweep confirms manipulation has occurred, but the move may not happen immediately after.
How to Verify: Look for equal highs/lows or obvious swing points where stops are likely placed. Did price just sweep (take out) these levels then quickly reverse? Look for a wick through the level followed by reversal candles.
DP Displacement (2 pts)

Strong, impulsive price movement (usually 1.5x+ ATR) indicating institutional participation. Shows smart money is aggressively entering the market in your direction.

Why 2 Points: Displacement shows aggression and commitment from institutions. While useful confirmation, it often occurs AFTER the best entry. It's supporting evidence that smart money is active, but shouldn't be the primary entry trigger.
How to Verify: Look for large-bodied candles (2x or more the size of recent candles) with small wicks. These "displacement" candles show aggressive institutional buying/selling. Recent displacement in your trade direction = confirmation.
TM Session Timing (3 pts)

ICT Kill Zones: London (2-5 AM EST), NY AM (7-10 AM), NY PM (1-3 PM). These are high-volume periods when institutions are most active.

Why 3 Points: Timing is CRITICAL in ICT. 80% of significant moves happen during kill zones. Trading outside these windows means low volume and choppy price action. The same setup during NY AM has 2-3x higher success rate than Asian session.
How to Verify: Check current time in EST. Best setups occur during: London Open (2-5 AM EST), NY AM Session (7-10 AM EST), or NY PM Session (1-3 PM EST). The session badge in the header shows if you're in a kill zone.
ML ML Model Confirmation (2 pts)

Machine learning model confirmation based on historical patterns. Adds statistical validation to your ICT analysis. Higher confidence = stronger confluence.

Why 2 Points: ML adds statistical edge by analyzing 50+ features from historical data. It's supplementary - ICT concepts should drive your decision, ML just provides additional confidence. Never rely on ML alone; use it to filter or confirm ICT setups.
How to Verify: Check the Dashboard page for ML signal and confidence %. If ML agrees with ICT direction (both say BUY or both say SELL) with >60% confidence, you have ML confirmation. The model uses 50+ features including ICT patterns.

20+ Points Strong Setup - High probability trade
15-19 Points Moderate Setup - Consider with caution
<15 Points Weak Setup - Wait for more confluence

ML Model Data & Features

The ML model uses XGBoost classification to predict whether the price will go UP or DOWN. Here's how it works:

1. Input Data (OHLCV)

The model receives raw OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) candlestick data:

Field Description
openOpening price of the candle
highHighest price during the candle
lowLowest price during the candle
closeClosing price of the candle
volumeTrading volume during the candle

2. Features Generated (~60+ features)

The FeatureEngineer class transforms OHLCV data into 60+ technical indicators:

Technical Indicators

Feature Description
rsi_14, rsi_7Relative Strength Index (momentum)
macd, macd_signal, macd_histMACD indicator and histogram
bb_upper, bb_middle, bb_lower, bb_width, bb_pctBollinger Bands (volatility)
ema_9, ema_21, ema_50Exponential Moving Averages
sma_20, sma_50Simple Moving Averages
ema_cross_9_21, ema_cross_21_50EMA crossover signals (0 or 1)
atr_14, atr_pctAverage True Range (volatility)
adx, di_plus, di_minusADX trend strength indicator
stoch_k, stoch_dStochastic Oscillator
cciCommodity Channel Index
obv, obv_smaOn Balance Volume
volume_sma_20, volume_ratioVolume relative to average
vwapVolume Weighted Average Price

Price Features

Feature Description
returns_1, returns_5, returns_10, returns_20Price returns over N periods
log_returnsLogarithmic returns
volatility_10, volatility_20Rolling standard deviation of returns
momentum_10, momentum_20Price momentum (current - past)
roc_10, roc_20Rate of change
price_sma20_ratio, price_sma50_ratioPrice relative to moving averages
hl_rangeHigh-Low range as % of close
close_positionWhere close is within the candle (0-1)
gapGap between open and previous close

Time Features

Feature Description

Lagged Features

macd_hist_lag_1, macd_hist_lag_2, macd_hist_lag_3, macd_hist_lag_5Past MACD histogram
volume_ratio_lag_1, volume_ratio_lag_2, volume_ratio_lag_3, volume_ratio_lag_5Past volume ratios

3. Target Variable

The model predicts a binary classification target:

target = 1 if future_return > 0 (price goes UP)
target = 0 if future_return <= 0 (price goes DOWN)

Where future_return = close[next_candle] / close[current_candle] - 1

4. How Prediction Works

1
Data Collection

Get latest OHLCV candles from exchange

2
Feature Engineering

Generate 60+ features from raw data

3
Model Prediction

XGBoost predicts probability of price going UP

4
Signal Generation
  • If probability >= 60%: BUY
  • If probability <= 40%: SELL
  • Otherwise: HOLD
5
Confluence

ML signal is combined with other confluence factors (ICT, SMT, MTF, Momentum) to make final trading decision

5. ML Weight in Confluence

The ML prediction contributes 15% to the overall trading signal confluence score:

ICT Strategy 40%

ML Prediction 15%
